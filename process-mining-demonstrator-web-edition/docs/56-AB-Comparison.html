A/B Comparison

Put two filtered process views side by side — A-Chart and B-Chart — and compare them directly: two segments, two periods, or the real process against a simulation. Sync the viewports, copy layouts, and read a live similarity score between the panels.

A/B Comparison shows two independent maps at once. Choose what each side loads (Original data, a sample, or a stored simulation), give each its own filters and metric, then use the valve to synchronise the viewports and the Similarity badge to measure how alike the two processes really are. A is always the master.

Pick the active side, then filter it

The sidebar filters, metric, date slider and presets act on the active side. Switch it by tapping a panel header or the A | B segmented control at the top of the Filters section — the active panel shows a pencil and an “editing” label.

AB ← the active side gets every sidebar setting
  • Each panel keeps its own metric pill, date window and Presets picker — so A and B can use entirely different settings at once.
  • Apply reloads only the active side.

Choose each side’s data source

4

The valve — sync or free the viewports

The ⇄ valve on the divider ties the two viewports together. Open: pan, zoom, reset and node drags mirror across both panels instantly. Closed: each panel moves independently (closing snapshots A’s view into B so they start aligned). A is always the master.

⇄ Valve open — viewports synced ⇄ Valve closed — independent

Open it to inspect the same region at the same zoom on both sides; close it to explore each map on its own.

5

Copy A’s layout to B

The ⧉ Copy layout button in A’s header transfers A’s complete viewport — zoom, pan and every node position — to B in one tap. Handy after you’ve hand-arranged A and want B laid out identically for a fair, node-for-node visual comparison.

6

Read the Similarity badge

A Similarity badge floats between the panels, showing Q(A, B) from 0 to 1 — how behaviourally alike the two processes are. It refreshes automatically whenever you change a filter or date on either side.

⇄ Similarity 0.56
green ≥ 0.70 — very similar blue 0.31–0.69 — partial red ≤ 0.30 — different

The colour is decided by these thresholds. See the Process Similarity guide for exactly how Q is calculated.

Good to know

  • Fully independent panels. Each side has its own filters, metric, KPI strip and Presets — so you can apply different presets to A and B at the same time.
  • A is the master. Both the valve sync and Copy layout flow from A to B.
  • Process Goodness in A/B. Each panel’s Process Goodness KPI tile is green when this side beats the other, red when lower, blue when equal within 0.005.
  • Compare reality with a what-if. Point one side at a stored Simulation (Sim-A / Sim-B) and the other at the Original data to see the effect of a change before you make it.

A/B Comparison turns “these two look different” into something you can see side by side and measure — with one number, the similarity score, on top.
